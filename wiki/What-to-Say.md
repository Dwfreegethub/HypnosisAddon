# What to Say

Almost nothing in this add-on is a command. You **speak**, in ordinary chat, and the subject's
client decides whether anything lands.

## The three rules

**Say their name.** Every suggestion requires the subject's name (or nickname) somewhere in the
line. Without it, nothing happens — this is what keeps ordinary conversation inert.

**Contractions and punctuation do not matter.** *"You can't move"*, *"you cannot move"* and
*"Missy — you CAN'T move!"* are all the same line.

**Talking about yourself is ignored.** A line starting "I" or "we" with no "you" in it never fires.
*"I kneel beside you"* will not make anyone kneel.

## The suggestions

Each needs its permission, and most need a minimum depth. **Releases** are listed in italics — they
skip the permission check, because revoking should never be harder than applying.

### Movement and posture

- *"you cannot move"* · *"stay still"* · *"you are frozen"* — **Movement Restriction**, Yielding
- *"kneel"* · *"on your knees"* — **Posture**, Yielding
- *"stand"* · *"get up"* · *"on your feet"* — **Posture**, Yielding
- *"you can move again"* · *"your body is your own"*

### Speech

- *"you cannot speak"* · *"stay silent"* · *"not a word"* — **Speech Restriction**, Yielding
- *"you can speak again"* · *"your voice is back"*

Note: this blocks ordinary chat. It cannot reach slash commands, so the safeword stays available.

### Clothing

- *"you cannot change your clothes"* · *"leave your clothes alone"* — **Clothing Restriction**,
  Yielding
- *"take something off"* · *"undress"* — **Undressing**, Entranced
- *"take everything off"* · *"strip"* — **Undressing**, Entranced
- *"you can change your clothes"* · *"your clothes are yours again"*

### Arousal and orgasm — **Arousal & Orgasm**, Entranced

- *"you are not aroused"* · *"your arousal fades"*
- *"you are lightly aroused"* · *"you feel a little warm"*
- *"you are very aroused"* · *"you are desperate"* · *"you need it badly"*
- *"you are right on the edge"* · *"you are so close"*
- *"you cannot come"* / *"you cannot cum"* — denial
- *"you may come now"* / *"you may cum now"* — lifts denial
- *"come for me"* / *"cum for me"* — forces it

A real chastity item or edging craft still refuses a forced orgasm. The add-on delegates that
decision to the game rather than reimplementing it.

- *"you cannot feel my touch"* · *"you feel nothing when I touch you"* — numbness
- *"you can feel my touch again"*

Numbness is different from not noticing: numb means the arousal genuinely does not land, not that
the message is hidden.

### Not noticing things — the awareness family

- *"you will not notice being undressed"* — **Not noticing clothing changes**, Drifting
- *"you will not notice the ropes"* · *"you do not notice being tied"* — **Not noticing bondage**,
  Drifting
- *"you will ignore my touches"* — **Not noticing touches**, Drifting
- *"you notice nothing"* · *"you are unaware"* — all three at once
- *"you notice everything again"* — releases all of them, and the clothing illusion with them

### The clothing illusion — **Clothing Illusion**, Deep, earned only

- *"you cannot tell what you are wearing"* · *"your clothes look the same to you"*
- *"you notice your clothes"* · *"look at yourself"*

Their own screen freezes on the clothes they were wearing when it took hold. The room sees the
truth.

### Blocking self-touch — **Self-Touch Control**, Yielding

- *"you cannot touch your breasts"* — around 40 body words are understood
- *"you cannot touch yourself"* — everything at once

### Ending the trance — no permission needed, ever

- *"wake up"* · *"you are awake"* · *"come back to me"*
- *"walk with me"* — walking trance · *"be still"* — back to stillness

## Checking a phrase

`/hypno match <phrase>` tells you whether the wording matched, and reports the name gate separately.
It is the fastest way to tell "my phrasing was wrong" from "something else refused it".

The in-game **What to Say** tab is generated directly from the pattern library, so it can never fall
behind the code. This page is hand-written and covers the common wordings; if something here does
not work, check the in-game tab.
