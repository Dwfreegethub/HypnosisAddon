# Drinks and Cigarettes

A crafted drink or cigarette can make you **easier** or **harder** to hypnotize for a while. The effect wears off on its own, and it never changes your trust in anyone.

**It is off until you turn it on.** On the **Depth** tab, set **Chemicals count** to *Arousal + drugs* or *Drugs only*. On the default, *Arousal only*, a tagged drink does nothing and ECHS tells you why.

---

## Making one

Craft the item in Bondage Club's crafting screen and put a tag in its **name or description**:

| Tag | What it does |
|---|---|
| `[hypno:relax]` | Lowers your guard: an induction lands more easily and goes deeper. |
| `[hypno:stimulate]` | Sharpens you: harder to put under, and easier to fight your way back up. |

Capital letters don't matter, so `[Hypno:Relax]` works too. Anyone can craft one, and the person handing you the drink doesn't need ECHS.

**Items that work:**
- **Drinks:** a crafted **Filled Glass** or **Mug**. It takes effect when someone uses the **Sip** action on your mouth with it. Sipping your own drink counts too.
- **Cigarettes:** a crafted **Cigarette**, either in your mouth slot or held in your hand with the **In Mouth** option. Held "In Hand", it does nothing.

---

## How strong, and for how long

| | Each dose | Goes up to |
|---|---|---|
| **Drink** | 15 a sip | 40 |
| **Cigarette** | 3 a minute while it's in your mouth | 20 |

- Once nothing is feeding it, the effect fades by 1 a minute.
- While a cigarette is still in your mouth, the effect doesn't fade.
- A cigarette never brings a drink's stronger effect back down.
- Relax and stimulate cancel each other out.
- **It adds to arousal.** Being aroused *and* relaxed is more than either alone, and a stimulant takes away from what arousal is doing.

**What it reaches:** the same things arousal does. That means whether an induction lands, how deep it goes, sinking deeper, and fighting back up. Like arousal, it **never** counts toward the three earned-only features (illusion, triggers, carry-forward) unless you opened those to chemicals yourself on the Depth tab.

---

## Checking it, and getting out

- **`/echs drugs`** shows how relaxed or stimulated you are right now, and whether it is counting.
- **`/echs safeword`** clears it at once, along with everything else. A cigarette still in your mouth won't start again until you take it out.
- Unticking **Hypnosis Enabled** clears it the same way.
- Refreshing the page clears it too. It is never saved.
