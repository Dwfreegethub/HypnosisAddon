**Mid-scene quick card**
* Stop everything: `/echs safeword`, or the popup's red button
* Wake them: *"Missy, wake up"*
* Let them move: *"Missy, you can move again"*
* Did my line match? `/echs match <line>`
* Am I deep enough? `/echs gates`
