# Depth and Trust

## Two questions, kept separate

A **permission** asks: *may they ever do this to me?*
**Depth** asks: *how far under do I have to be before it can happen?*

Both must be satisfied, every time. Granting a permission does not mean someone can use it now — it
means they can use it once they have got you deep enough.

## The five depths

| Tier | From | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Drifting** | 0 | barely under, still mostly present |
| **Yielding** | 20 | noticeably affected, starting to respond |
| **Entranced** | 40 | clearly hypnotized, suggestions land |
| **Deep** | 60 | will faded, limited self-direction |
| **Blank** | 80 | fully gone, complete receptivity |

## What each depth reaches

These are the **defaults**. Every one is adjustable on the Depth tab — you can move any feature up
or down for yourself. Deeper is a consent setting, not a difficulty.

**Drifting** — Not noticing clothing changes · Not noticing bondage changes · Not noticing touches

**Yielding** — Cannot move · Cannot speak · Posture · Cannot reach the wardrobe · Cannot touch
yourself · Made to act on yourself

**Entranced** — Follow / leash · Undressing · Arousal & orgasm

**Deep** — Clothing illusion\* · Planting triggers\* · Suggestions that outlive the trance\*

\* **earned depth only** — arousal cannot reach these by default.

Noticing less is the shallowest thing hypnosis does; things that outlive the session are the
deepest. That ordering is the design.

## Trust

Trust is **per person** and lives on **your** client. It is counted from your interactions with
them, not set by anyone.

An ordinary conversational message builds a little — at most one every five minutes, worth double
when they use your name. A successful induction is worth five of those.

Roughly: **minutes** to be reachable at all, **an evening** to be genuinely usable, **a long time**
to be deeply trusted.

The stored value is the interaction *count*, not the score. That means the curve behind it can be
retuned without corrupting what you have built with anyone.

## Arousal is a floor, not a multiplier

Your access is the **higher** of your trust and your arousal — and arousal is capped at **30**.

So being worked up lets a stranger reach the shallow, session-only things, and nothing deeper, ever.
A multiplier on zero trust would still be zero, which would give a stranger nothing; a floor is what
makes the mechanic mean anything for the case it exists for.

## The earned-only three

The clothing illusion, planting triggers, and carrying a suggestion past waking all need **earned**
depth — trust, not arousal — because two of them outlive the session and one lies to you about your
own body.

The Depth tab can open the **illusion** and **triggers** to arousal if you want, at the price of
fading fast: anything planted that way decays quickly whatever your decay setting says.
**Carry-forward stays earned-only** and cannot be opened, because it has no decay clock to price the
shortcut with.

## Relationships give a floor

Read from BC's own friend list, lovership and ownership:

| | Floor | Reach |
|---|---|---|
| **Friend** | 15 | gets in the door |
| **Lover** | 30 | also reaches arousal |
| **Owner** | 65 | reaches everything |

The floor sits *under* whatever you have earned, so a relationship is never re-earned and decay can
never take it.

## Trust fades without contact

Off by default. In the **Advanced** view (button under the tabs): Never · Very slowly · Slowly ·
Typical · Fast · Very fast.

Because the curve is steep at the bottom and flat at the top, a fixed number of missed interactions
costs a casual acquaintance far more than an established bond. That is intentional, and it is why
there is no second curve for it.

## The roll

When someone attempts hypnosis:

```
chance = access + your choice (Agree +25 / Fight −25) + experience + their honoured skill
```

clamped to **5–95**, so it is never certain in either direction. Depth falls out of the same roll —
a comfortable success goes deep, a squeaker leaves a shallow trance you can wake yourself from.

You get **two attempts** by default before a ten-minute cooldown. That count is your setting on the
Permissions tab; two or three.

`/hypno chance <name>` shows the real numbers for all three choices. It is the single most useful
diagnostic here, because it exposes the formula instead of making you infer it from outcomes.

## Their skill, and whether you believe it

Practised hypnotists are better at it. Their client tells yours how practised they are; **your**
Depth-tab setting decides how much of that to believe:

- ignore it entirely
- honour it only from people you already trust
- honour it up to a cap, from anyone

You feel it as a read on their manner at the prompt, never as a number. It can raise a roll, and it
can **never** reach the earned-only three.

## Experience

Your own experience with hypnosis is one pool, not two. Its sign follows your choice: it helps you
go under when you agree, and helps you resist when you fight. Practice is practice; what you point
it at is a per-attempt decision.
