# Sensory Modulation

> 🛡️ **EMERGENCY EXIT — ANY TIME, ANY STATE**  
> If you need to stop or end the scene immediately: **Type `/echs safeword`**.  
> Works 100% of the time, even while completely silenced. It instantly breaks active trances, releases sensory blocks, and restores full hearing and vision.

Narrowing what the subject can perceive through hypnotic suggestion. **Hearing and sight are built** (since v0.93.0). A softer "muffled" hearing is planned and not built yet.

---

## Hearing Only One Voice

Requires the **Hearing** permission (Permissions tab, off by default) and an **Entranced** trance (40 by default; adjustable on your Depth tab).

| The hypnotist says | What she hears |
|---|---|
| *"Missy, you hear only my voice"* · *"you will only hear me"* · *"my voice is the only one you can hear"* | Everything that one person says, named or not. Nobody else. |
| *"Missy, you only hear what is said to you"* · *"you only hear your name"* | Only lines that use her name, and whispers to her, from anyone. |
| *"Missy, you can hear everyone again"* · *"your hearing comes back"* | Everyone, as normal (release). |

### What Still Gets Through
* Everything she can **see**: emotes, activities, items being placed or removed, people arriving and leaving.
* **Out-of-character text in (parentheses)**, in chat or whispers, from anyone. The rest of that line is hidden; the OOC part is shown on its own. This is the safety line: anyone can still check in with *"(are you ok?)"*.
* Her own chat lines, and everything ECHS tells her.

### What Is Hidden
Everyone else's public chat and in-character whispers. Now and then, at most once a minute, an atmospheric line takes their place: *"Other voices murmur somewhere far away. They don't matter."*

Bondage Club's own deafness cannot do this: at any level it only garbles speech, and it has no way to let one voice through. ECHS hides the lines itself, before BC's deafness and before BC's chat log, so a hidden line is not in her log either.

### What She Can't Hear Can't Reach Her
While it is on, other people's spoken commands and trigger words do nothing to her. In the "her name" version, a line that names her is heard, so it works as normal. A hypnotist she can't hear can still use the buttons on their ECHS panel, such as **Wake**.

### Duration & Exits
* **Spoken in a trance:** until the trance ends, the release, or her safeword. It is not carried by *"that will stay with you"*.
* **As a trigger:** *"my voice"* means **whoever says the trigger word**, and it lasts as long as her trigger effects do (the *Minutes a fired trigger lasts* setting on her Triggers tab).
* **`/echs safeword` always ends it.**

## Sight

Requires the **Sight** permission (Permissions tab, off by default) and an **Entranced** trance (40 by default; adjustable on your Depth tab).

| The hypnotist says | What she sees |
|---|---|
| *"your vision is dimming"* · *"the room grows dim"* | Dim: her screen at about a third of its brightness (BC's light blindness) |
| *"you can barely see"* · *"everything is going dark"* | Very dark (BC's normal blindness) |
| *"you cannot see"* · *"you are blind"* · *"everything is fading to black"* | Black (BC's heavy blindness) |
| *"you can see again"* · *"your vision clears"* | Normal sight (release) |

### Powered by Bondage Club's Own Blindness
It is BC's blindness, not an overlay of ours, so BC's limits and her own BC settings always win:
* **Her Sensory Deprivation setting.** On **Light**, BC stops at "very dark", even for *"you cannot see"*. The hypnotist is told it stopped short, and she is told some light stays with her.
* **Everything BC does with blindness follows her settings:** the darkened screen; *Blind Adjacent* (only the people next to her are visible); *Blind Disable Examine*; names hidden under her Sensory Deprivation level; a shorter sight range on a map; and struggling out of items is harder, as under any blindfold.
* **A real blindfold adds to it**, by BC's own rules, and the cap still holds.
* **Nobody else sees any change.** Her appearance is not touched; no blindfold appears on her unless one is really put on her.

The trance's own soft white veil steps aside while her sight is dimmed or gone, so the dark is not washed grey, and comes back when she can see again. A real blindfold worn in a trance does the same.

### Duration & Exits
Until the trance ends, the release, or her safeword. Unlike hearing, *"that will stay with you"* can carry it past the wake, and it can be planted in a trigger. Unticking **Sight** ends it at once. Reloading the page mid-trance clears it, as it does "you cannot speak".
