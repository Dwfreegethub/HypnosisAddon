# Sensory Modulation

What the subject can perceive, narrowed by suggestion. **Hearing is built** (v0.93.0). Blindness, and a softer "muffled" hearing, are planned and not built yet.

## Hearing only one voice

Needs the **Hearing** permission (Permissions tab, off by default) and an **Entranced** trance.

| The hypnotist says | What she hears |
|---|---|
| *"Missy, you hear only my voice"* | Everything that one person says, named or not. Nobody else. |
| *"Missy, you only hear what is said to you"* | Only lines that use her name, from anyone. |
| *"Missy, you can hear everyone again"* | Everyone, as normal. |

**Still gets through:**
* Everything she can **see**: emotes, activities, items, people arriving and leaving.
* **Out-of-character text in (parentheses)**, in chat or whispers, from anyone. The rest of that line is hidden; the OOC part is shown on its own. This is the safety line: anyone can still ask *"(are you ok?)"*.
* Her own lines, and everything ECHS tells her.

**Hidden:** everyone else's chat and in-character whispers. Now and then, at most once a minute, a line takes their place: *"Other voices murmur somewhere far away. They don't matter."*

**What she can't hear can't reach her.** While it is on, other people's commands and trigger words do nothing to her. In the "her name" version, a line that names her is heard, so it works as normal. A hypnotist she can't hear can still use the buttons on their ECHS panel, such as **Wake**.

**How long it lasts:**
* Spoken in a trance: until the trance ends, the release, or her safeword. It is not carried by *"that will stay with you"*.
* As a trigger: *"my voice"* means **whoever says the trigger word**, and it lasts as long as her trigger effects do (the *Minutes a fired trigger lasts* setting on her Triggers tab).
* **`/echs safeword` always ends it.**

Bondage Club's own deafness cannot do this: at any level it only garbles speech, and it has no way to let one voice through. ECHS hides the lines itself, before BC's deafness and before BC's chat log, so a hidden line is not in her log either.
